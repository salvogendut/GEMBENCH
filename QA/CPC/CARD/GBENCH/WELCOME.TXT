GEOBENCH
A GEOS/Workbench-style desktop for
the Amstrad CPC.

You are reading this file in VIEWER,
the first GEOBENCH app written in C.
It runs in an expansion bank and asks
the kernel to load this file, then
draws the text through the same API
the assembly apps use.

Double-click a file on Disk A to open
it; press ESC or click to close.

Enjoy exploring!
